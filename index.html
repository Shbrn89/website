02

SUMMARLY-LEGAL — Legal Document Summarization

Summarize Long Terms & Conditions Documents

A text summarization system for long Terms & Conditions documents using a TF-IDF extractive baseline and Transformer-based models, with BART as the main trained model and T5/LED as comparison models.

What I built

  • TF-IDF Baseline — Extractive summarization that selects important sentences based on word weights.
  • BART Trained Model — Main abstractive Transformer model loaded from a local training checkpoint.
  • T5 & LED — Comparison models used to evaluate summarization performance against BART and the TF-IDF baseline.
